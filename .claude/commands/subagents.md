Use subagents to break down complex tasks into smaller, more manageable parts and run the subagents in parallel.

## Subagent 1: Task Analysis

- Analyze the task and break it down into smaller, more manageable parts.
- Identify the key components of the task.
- Identify the dependencies between the components.
- Identify the constraints on the task.
- Identify the resources required to complete the task.
- Identify the risks associated with the task.
- Identify the mitigations for the risks.
